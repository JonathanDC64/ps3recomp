# Step C3a: the shader texts and compiles. The core's copies of the text
# builders and of the .dxbc cache are gone; it calls rsxc_shader.c's texts
# and gpu_shader.c's compile service, whose dxbc target is today's FXC call
# and cache (the same keys and files). A legacy fix in one of these regions
# goes to rsxc_shader.c or gpu_shader.c (their lines above), not to the
# fragment. rsxc_d3d12.c includes rsxc_shadercache.inc right after
# rsxc_state.inc, before the first fragment that compiles.
#  rsxc_head.inc 143-152: rsx_path_last_sep is gpu_shader.c's.
#  rsxc_shadercache.inc 3955-3966, 3970-4148: the cache, its key, store,

#   s_perf_fxc_n and s_perf_sc_hits are its counters (gpu_sc_stats_take;
